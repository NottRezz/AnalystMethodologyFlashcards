Final Interview Cheat Sheet

                    
Agile/Scrum: how teams build iteratively, get feedback, and adapt.

                    
ITIL 4: how IT services are managed, supported, and improved.

                    
Change Management: how updates are introduced safely without breaking live systems.

                    
Best Practices: staging, testing, version control, reviews, monitoring, logging, documentation, backups, and rollback plans.

                    
System Analyst: bridge between business needs and technical solutions.

                    

                    
Use this if you blank:

                    
“Agile and Scrum help teams build in small, feedback-driven cycles. ITIL helps manage and support services once they are running. Change management controls risk when updates are introduced, and industry best practices keep everything reliable, documented, tested, and maintainable.”
